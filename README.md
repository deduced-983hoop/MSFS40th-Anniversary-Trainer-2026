## 🎮 About the Game

Microsoft Flight Simulator is a civilian aviation simulation game developed by Asobo Studio and published by Xbox Game Studios. The Windows version launched on August 18, 2020, and the free 40th Anniversary Edition update released on November 11, 2022. It features realistic aircraft systems, global scenery, live weather, navigation, flight planning, helicopters, gliders, and historical aircraft.

## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **Microsoft Flight Simulator (2020) 40th Anniversary Edition**. A Windows PC trainer for Microsoft Flight Simulator 40th Anniversary Edition that provides optional control over common flight-simulation conditions. It focuses on flight practice and convenience by managing fuel, damage protection, simulation pause, position recovery, time settings, and selected aircraft-state options without adding weapons, missions, or unsupported gameplay systems.

**Target Audience:** PC players who want to practice aircraft handling, navigation, cockpit procedures, helicopter and glider operations, sightseeing, or difficult approaches with fewer penalties and less repetition.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Microsoft Flight Simulator models aircraft movement through pitch, roll, yaw, throttle, trim, flaps, landing gear, brakes, and aircraft-specific flight controls. Use the trainer for recovery or practice assistance rather than replacing normal control inputs. Slew mode and simulator control bindings remain useful for repositioning an aircraft during testing.

### Tools & Inventory

The game has no conventional inventory system. The relevant resources are aircraft fuel, payload, flight-plan data, and cockpit system states. The trainer focuses on fuel availability and aircraft conditions instead of items or equipment management.

### World & Environment

Flights take place across a detailed global environment with airports, navigational facilities, live or preset weather, time-of-day settings, air traffic, terrain, and points of interest. Use the trainer to explore routes, repeat approaches, and practice in conditions that would otherwise require restarting a flight.

### Combat & Weapons

Microsoft Flight Simulator is a civilian flight simulator and has no standard combat, weapon, or enemy system. The trainer does not add combat mechanics. Aircraft handling, navigation, emergency procedures, landing practice, and sightseeing are the primary gameplay activities.
